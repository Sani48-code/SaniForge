## Challenge

A growing B2B sales team was capturing leads from three different web forms across their site, but every lead had to be manually copy-pasted from an email notification into their CRM. With no consistent process, leads sat unassigned for hours, duplicate entries piled up, and at least a few leads a month were quietly lost when someone forgot to forward the notification email.

The team estimated they were spending close to a full workday each week just moving lead data between inboxes, spreadsheets, and the CRM — time that could have gone toward actually following up.

## Approach

I designed and built an n8n workflow that replaced the entire manual chain with a single automated pipeline:

- Webhook triggers capture form submissions the moment they're sent, instead of waiting on email notifications.
- An enrichment step validates email and phone formatting, flags obvious spam/bot submissions, and appends company data where available.
- Validated leads are pushed directly into the CRM via API, with source, campaign, and timestamp fields mapped automatically.
- A duplicate-check node compares incoming leads against existing CRM records to prevent double entries.
- A Slack integration posts a real-time alert to the sales channel with the lead's details and a direct CRM link, so reps can respond within minutes instead of hours.

The build took about two weeks, including a testing phase where submissions were shadow-logged alongside the old manual process to confirm nothing was being missed before fully cutting over.

## Result

The sales team eliminated manual data entry entirely — every lead now lands in the CRM and Slack within seconds of submission. That freed up roughly 8 hours per week previously spent on copy-pasting and chasing down missed notifications. Response times to new leads dropped from hours to minutes, and the duplicate-check step has kept the CRM clean with zero leads lost to manual entry since launch.
