## Challenge

[Client Name]'s sales team manually copied leads from four-plus disconnected sources (web forms, an ad platform, a chat widget, and email) into their CRM, tagging and assigning each one by hand before sending a follow-up. That process cost over a full workday a week across the team, and leads were sometimes left untouched for 24 hours or more.

## Approach

I designed an automation pipeline that replaced the manual chain end to end:

- **Centralized Intake**: every lead source connected via webhook triggers into one automation hub.
- **Automatic Enrichment & Deduplication**: incoming leads checked against existing CRM records, with firmographic data appended automatically.
- **Smart Routing**: leads assigned by service type, territory, and current workload through a rules engine.
- **Instant Follow-Up**: an automated, personalized confirmation email sent immediately, paired with a real-time notification to the assigned rep, cutting response time to under 2 minutes.

## Results

| Metric | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manual hours/week on lead entry | 8 hrs | **0 hrs** |
| Average first-response time | 6.5 hrs | **2 min** |
| Duplicate lead records | ~15%/mo | **&lt;1%/mo** |
| Lead-to-meeting conversion rate | 11% | **19%** |

## Key Takeaways

- Speed-to-lead is a direct revenue lever. The drop in response time nearly doubled the conversion rate.
- Automation removes human error, not just human effort.
- The best automations are invisible to the team's existing workflow. Reps didn't have to learn a new tool.
