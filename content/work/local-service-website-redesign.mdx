## Challenge

A local restoration company relied almost entirely on their website for emergency service calls, but the site was slow, cluttered, and clearly built years earlier without mobile visitors in mind. Most of their traffic came from people searching on their phones during a stressful situation — a flooded basement or storm damage — and the site was making that worse: a multi-step contact form, buried phone number, and load times north of 6 seconds on mobile connections.

The business was losing potential jobs to competitors with simpler, faster sites, even when they ranked comparably in local search.

## Approach

The redesign focused on speed and lead-capture clarity above everything else:

- Rebuilt the site mobile-first, with the phone number and a single-field "request service" form visible without scrolling on every page.
- Consolidated service information into clear, dedicated pages per service type (water damage, fire damage, mold remediation) instead of one long catch-all page.
- Optimized all images and rebuilt the front end to cut unnecessary scripts, bringing mobile load time under 2 seconds.
- Simplified the lead form from six fields down to three, deferring detailed intake questions to the follow-up call instead of the form itself.
- Added click-to-call tracking and form-submission tracking so the client could see which channel was actually driving jobs.

The redesign and migration were completed over a six-week period, with the old site kept live in parallel until the new site's tracking confirmed stable performance.

## Result

Within 90 days of launch, lead form submissions tripled compared to the prior site, and mobile load time dropped to under 2 seconds. Click-to-call activity also rose noticeably once the phone number was made persistently visible. The client has since used the same page structure as a template for adding new service lines without needing another full redesign.
