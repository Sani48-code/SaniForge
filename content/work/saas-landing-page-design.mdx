## Challenge

[Client Name], an early-stage SaaS company, was driving real paid and organic traffic to a polished but underperforming homepage. Generic hero copy and stock photography buried the actual product, there were no screenshots above the fold, the primary CTA was a vague "Learn More," and the demo request form asked for a phone number and company size before anyone had a reason to trust the page.

## Approach

- **Clarity-First Hero Section**: a specific, benefit-driven headline paired with a real product screenshot instead of stock imagery.
- **Proof Stacked Early**: customer logos and a quantified result moved above the fold, next to the primary CTA.
- **One Clear Action**: every button on the page changed to a single, consistent "Book a Demo" CTA.
- **Frictionless Form**: the demo request form cut down to just name, work email, and company name.

## Key Takeaways

- Show the product, not a metaphor for the product.
- One CTA beats many. Competing calls to action dilute all of them.
- Every form field costs you conversions; ask for the minimum needed to start the conversation.
