## Challenge

A B2B SaaS company had a landing page that looked polished but wasn't converting. The hero section led with a vague tagline instead of a clear value proposition, the page loaded in close to 4 seconds on mobile, and calls-to-action were scattered inconsistently throughout the page instead of building toward one clear next step. Analytics showed heavy drop-off before visitors ever reached the demo booking form.

The client needed a rebuild that kept their brand identity but fixed the structural and performance problems actually costing them conversions.

## Approach

I rebuilt the page in Next.js with conversion and performance treated as equal priorities from the start:

- Rewrote the hero to lead with a specific, benefit-driven headline and a single primary CTA, removing competing links above the fold.
- Restructured the page flow around a clear hierarchy: problem framing, product proof, social proof (logos and a testimonial block), then a repeated, consistent CTA at every major scroll stop.
- Rebuilt image handling with next/image and lazy loading, trimmed unused JavaScript, and moved to static generation for the page shell to cut load time.
- Added scroll-depth and CTA-click tracking so the client could see exactly where visitors dropped off post-launch, not just guess.

The rebuild took roughly three weeks from wireframe to launch, including one round of A/B testing on the hero headline before locking the final version.

## Result

Page load time dropped from around 4 seconds to 1.2 seconds on mobile, directly reducing early bounce. Scroll-to-CTA completion — the percentage of visitors who reached and engaged with a CTA — rose by 38%. Combined, the clearer structure and faster load pushed demo booking rate to 2.4x its previous baseline within the first month post-launch, giving the client a page they could confidently send paid traffic to.
