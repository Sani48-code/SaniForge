## Challenge

A B2B SaaS client had a blog with dozens of posts published over the years, but almost none of it was driving organic traffic. Articles were written around whatever topic seemed interesting that week, with no shared keyword strategy, no internal linking plan, and no clear picture of what their actual buyers were searching for. Traffic was flat, and the client had no confidence that investing in more blog content would change that.

They wanted proof that content could actually move the needle before committing to a long-term content budget.

## Approach

Instead of another scattershot batch of posts, I built the series around a single keyword cluster strategy:

- Identified one core topic area with clear buyer intent and mapped 12 supporting keywords ranging from top-of-funnel questions to comparison and decision-stage searches.
- Structured every article around a specific search intent — informational, comparison, or transactional — rather than writing generic overviews.
- Built a deliberate internal linking structure so each article funneled authority and readers toward a central pillar page and relevant product pages.
- Optimized each post for on-page SEO fundamentals: title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, and image alt text, with no reliance on paid promotion.

All 12 articles were researched, written, and published over a 3-month window, with rankings tracked weekly to catch and fix any underperforming pages early.

## Result

All 12 articles in the series reached Google's first page for their target keywords, with several holding top-5 positions within the first two months. The cluster added an estimated 2,400 monthly organic visits on average once rankings stabilized, giving the client a clear, repeatable model for content investment going forward. The internal linking structure also lifted rankings for the pillar page the series supported, compounding the return beyond the 12 articles themselves.
