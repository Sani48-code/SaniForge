## Challenge

[Client Name] had 40+ published blog posts generating almost no organic traffic, mostly ranking page 4 or beyond. The posts were written for the internal team rather than for what customers actually searched, with no keyword research process, no content calendar, and no clear tie to business goals.

## Approach

Instead of another scattershot batch of posts, I built the series around a single keyword cluster strategy:

- **Keyword & Intent Research**: identifying 12 keyword opportunities, prioritizing buyer intent over vanity search volume.
- **Topic Cluster Structure**: grouping the 12 articles into three clusters, each anchored by a pillar page with deliberate internal linking.
- **Depth Over Volume**: writing every article to be the genuinely best answer available (1,500-2,500 words), drawing on subject-matter expert insight rather than generic overviews.
- **On-Page SEO Discipline**: every article shipped with optimized title tags, meta descriptions, header hierarchy, internal links, and schema.

## Results

| Metric | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Articles ranking page 1 | 0 of 12 | **12 of 12** |
| Average keyword position | #47 | **#6** |
| Organic blog traffic | 210 sessions/mo | **3,400 sessions/mo** |
| Blog-attributed leads | ~1/mo | **~18/mo** |

## Key Takeaways

- 12 focused articles outperformed 40 unfocused ones.
- Topic clusters build compounding authority instead of 12 isolated bets.
- Search intent match matters more than word count. Depth only pays off when it's aimed at the right question.
