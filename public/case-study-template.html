Our Approach

A 3-Stage Composable Workflow Pipeline

Rather than replacing human lawyers with unchecked AI, we built a human-in-the-loop review architecture. The AI performs structured research and drafting, leaving attorneys with only a 3-minute final verification step.

01

Structured Queue Ingestion

Extracted priority target keywords and practice areas directly into an automated n8n webhook queue.

02

AI Research & Legal Prompt Tuning

Tuned LLM prompts with strict statutory disclaimers, state-specific legal references, and structured heading hierarchies.

03

Automated Publishing & Schema Injection

Formatted HTML with FAQ schema markup, meta titles, internal links, and pushed directly to WordPress staged drafts.

Key Takeaways

  • ✓ Automate the mechanical, protect the strategic: High-end professional firms maintain quality by automating content research, formatting, and drafting while retaining human sign-off.
  • ✓ Composable pipelines beat monolithic apps: n8n combined with LLM models allows custom business validation and easy schema updates.
  • ✓ SEO topical authority requires cluster depth: Publishing complete clusters quickly signals immediate authority to Google compared to sporadic monthly blogging.
